## [meta] Meta (Facebook and Instagram), paraphrased
Ads must not contain deceptive or misleading content. Alcohol, dating and cryptocurrency ads are restricted. Targeting by sensitive personal attributes is not allowed, and housing, employment and credit ads have restricted targeting.

## [google-ads] Google Ads, paraphrased
Ads must not promote counterfeit goods or dangerous products. Editorial standards reject gimmicky capitalisation and punctuation. Healthcare, gambling and financial services are restricted categories.

## [tiktok] TikTok, paraphrased
Ads must not make misleading claims or use fake endorsements, and political advertising is not allowed in most markets.

## [linkedin] LinkedIn, paraphrased
Ads must be professional in tone and must not use deceptive tactics. Job ads must not discriminate.
