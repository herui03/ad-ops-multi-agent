## [superlatives] Superlative and ranking claims
topic: superlative-claims
position: prohibited-unless-substantiated
Ad copy must not use unqualified superlatives or rankings such as "best", "No.1", "number one", "only", "top" or "most". A superlative is allowed only when the advertiser holds dated, independent evidence and names the source in the ad. Creatives that break this rule are blocked until revised.

## [price-claims] Price and discount claims
topic: price-claims
position: validity-period-required
Any price, discount or "from" price shown in an ad must state the validity period and the main conditions in the ad itself. Percentage discounts must refer to a real previous price. Price claims without a validity period are blocked.

## [guarantees] Guaranteed outcomes
topic: guaranteed-outcomes
position: prohibited
Ads must not promise guaranteed results, such as "guaranteed returns", "100% effective" or "guaranteed upgrade". Guarantee language is blocked regardless of placement.

## [text-in-image] Text overlay on images
topic: text-in-image
position: max-20-percent
Text overlaid on an image creative must cover no more than 20 percent of the image area. Creatives above the limit are rejected at upload.

## [finance-disclaimer] Financial products
topic: finance-disclaimer
position: risk-warning-required
Ads for investment, lending or insurance products must show a risk warning in the ad copy. Past performance must not be presented as a promise of future results.

## [alcohol] Alcohol
topic: alcohol-targeting
position: age-21-plus
Alcohol ads may only be targeted to users aged 21 and above and must not suggest that drinking improves social, sexual or physical performance.

## [sensitive-targeting] Sensitive audience targeting
topic: sensitive-targeting
position: prohibited
Advertisers must not target or exclude audiences by race, religion, health condition or sexual orientation, and must not imply knowledge of such attributes in ad copy.

## [landing-page] Landing pages
topic: landing-page
position: privacy-policy-required
Landing pages must load on mobile, show a privacy policy link and identify the advertiser's registered business name.

## [launch-approval] Campaign launch approval
topic: launch-approval
position: human-approver-required
In the SimAds sandbox every campaign launch or budget change requires an explicit decision by a person holding the approver role. Launch requests that have not been approved are never executed.
