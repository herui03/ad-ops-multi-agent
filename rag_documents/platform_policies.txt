# Social Media Platform Advertising Policies

## Meta (Facebook & Instagram)
### Prohibited Content
- Ads must not contain deceptive, false, or misleading content, including deceptive claims, offers, or methods.
- Ads must not contain content that exploits crises or controversial political or social issues for commercial purposes.
- Ads must not promote the sale or use of illegal products or services.
- Ads must not contain adult content, including nudity or implied sexual activity.
- Ads must not promote payday loans or any loan with an APR of 36% or higher.

### Restricted Content (requires special approval)
- Alcohol ads: Must comply with local laws and target age 18+ (or legal drinking age).
- Dating ads: Must be pre-approved and follow dating-specific guidelines.
- Cryptocurrency ads: Require written approval from Meta.
- Political ads: Must include "Paid for by" disclaimers and be registered in the Ad Library.
- Health & wellness: Cannot make unrealistic promises about results.

### Targeting Restrictions
- Cannot target ads based on sensitive personal attributes (race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation).
- Special Ad Categories (housing, employment, credit) have restricted targeting options.
- Custom audiences must be collected with user consent.
- Lookalike audiences for Special Ad Categories are limited.

### Technical Requirements
- Image ads: Primary text 125 characters, headline 27 characters recommended.
- Video ads: Recommended aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5 for feed.
- Text in images should be minimal (old 20% text rule replaced by algorithmic preference).
- Landing pages must be functional and match ad content.

## Google Ads
### Prohibited Content
- Counterfeit goods are strictly prohibited.
- Dangerous products or services (explosives, drugs, weapons).
- Content enabling dishonest behavior (hacking software, fake documents).
- Inappropriate content (bullying, discrimination, shocking content).

### Restricted Content
- Sexual content: Limited, with strict targeting requirements.
- Alcohol: Allowed in specific countries, must comply with local laws.
- Gambling: Requires certification and is limited to specific countries.
- Healthcare: Restrictions vary by country; pharmaceutical ads require certification.
- Financial services: Must comply with local regulations, include required disclaimers.

### Editorial Standards
- Ads must be clearly written and professional.
- No excessive capitalization (e.g., "FREE MONEY NOW").
- No gimmicky use of punctuation or symbols.
- Phone numbers in ad text are not allowed (use call extensions instead).
- Landing page must be functional, easy to navigate, and relevant to the ad.

### Technical Requirements
- Responsive search ads: Up to 15 headlines (30 chars each), 4 descriptions (90 chars each).
- Display ads: Multiple size formats required for broad reach.
- Video ads (YouTube): 15-second non-skippable or 6-second bumper ads available.

## TikTok
### Prohibited Content
- No misleading claims or fake endorsements.
- No promotion of tobacco, drugs, or weapons.
- No political advertising in most markets.
- No content that infringes intellectual property rights.
- No ads promoting excessive body modification or unrealistic beauty standards.

### Content Guidelines
- Ads must feel native to the TikTok experience.
- Overly polished or corporate-feeling content performs poorly and may be deprioritized.
- Sound-on is expected — ads without audio are disadvantaged.
- Vertical video (9:16) is required for full-screen experience.
- Hook within the first 2 seconds is critical for engagement.

### Restricted Content
- Financial services: Must include risk disclaimers and target 18+.
- Gambling: Prohibited in most markets.
- Alcohol: Allowed in select markets with age gating.
- Health supplements: Claims must be substantiated and not misleading.

### Technical Requirements
- Video length: 5-60 seconds (9-15 seconds recommended).
- Resolution: Minimum 720p, recommended 1080p.
- File size: Up to 500MB.
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (full screen vertical).

## LinkedIn
### Professional Standards
- All ads must maintain professional tone appropriate to a business context.
- No sensationalized or clickbait headlines.
- No content irrelevant to professional development or business.
- Job ads must represent real, currently open positions.

### Prohibited Content
- Deceptive business practices or get-rich-quick schemes.
- Multi-level marketing (MLM) recruitment.
- Adult content or dating services.
- Illegal products or services.

### Restricted Content
- Financial services must include required regulatory disclaimers.
- Educational institutions must be accredited.
- Political ads are restricted to certain markets.
- Health-related ads must be substantiated.

### Technical Requirements
- Sponsored Content: 150 characters intro text, 70 characters headline.
- Image: 1200x627 pixels recommended.
- Video: 3 seconds to 30 minutes, 16:9 or 1:1 aspect ratio.
- Carousel: 2-10 cards per ad.
