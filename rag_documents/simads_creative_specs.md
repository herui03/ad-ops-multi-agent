## [image-sizes] Image sizes
Feed image creatives are 1080 by 1080 pixels or 1080 by 1350 pixels, JPG or PNG, under 5 MB.

## [text-overlay] Text overlay allowance
topic: text-in-image
position: max-30-percent
Text overlays on feed images may cover up to 30 percent of the image area when the text is legible on a mobile screen.

## [video-length] Video length
Short-video creatives run between 6 and 30 seconds. The first 3 seconds should show the brand or offer.

## [headline-length] Headline length
Headlines should be 40 characters or fewer so they are not truncated in the feed.
