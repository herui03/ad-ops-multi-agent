## [voice] Brand voice
Professional yet approachable; explain complex technology in human terms. Never use "revolutionary", "game-changing" or "disrupting". Preferred terms are "intelligent", "streamlined", "measurable impact" and "evidence-based".

## [visual-identity] Visual identity
Primary colours are Deep Navy and Electric Teal. Never use red as a primary colour. The logo needs at least 24 pixels of clear space and must not sit on busy backgrounds.

## [statistics] Statistics in ads
Statistics must be sourced and dated in the ad, for example "NovaByte internal study, Q3 2024". Financial ROI claims must link to the case study source.

## [dos-donts] Advertising dos and don'ts
Use real customer testimonials with attribution and a clear call to action. Do not run the same creative for more than 4 weeks without a refresh, and do not bid on competitors' brand keywords.
