# NovaByte Technologies — Brand Guidelines

## Company Overview
NovaByte Technologies is a B2B SaaS company specializing in AI-powered analytics for mid-market enterprises. Founded in 2021, headquartered in Singapore with offices in London and San Francisco.

## Brand Voice
- Tone: Professional yet approachable. We explain complex technology in human terms.
- We are: Confident, innovative, trustworthy, data-driven.
- We are NOT: Aggressive, hype-driven, condescending, overly casual.
- Never use: "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "disrupting" — these are overused and erode trust.
- Preferred terms: "Intelligent", "streamlined", "measurable impact", "evidence-based".

## Visual Identity
- Primary colors: Deep Navy (#1B2A4A), Electric Teal (#00C9A7).
- Secondary colors: Soft Gray (#F4F5F7), Warm White (#FAFBFC).
- Never use red as a primary color — it conflicts with our trust-oriented positioning.
- Logo must have minimum 24px clear space on all sides.
- Logo must not be placed on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.

## Messaging Framework
- Hero message: "Turn your data into decisions."
- Supporting messages:
  1. "AI analytics that speak your language."
  2. "From insight to action in minutes, not months."
  3. "Built for teams who measure what matters."
- Always lead with the customer benefit, not the feature.
- Statistics must be sourced and dated (e.g., "87% faster reporting — NovaByte Internal Study, Q3 2024").

## Advertising Dos and Don'ts
### Do:
- Use real customer testimonials with attribution.
- Include clear CTAs (Call-to-Action) in every ad.
- A/B test all headlines before scaling spend.
- Target by job title and industry, not demographics.
- Show product UI screenshots in display ads where possible.

### Don't:
- Make claims we can't substantiate with data.
- Use stock photos of people — use product screenshots or abstract visuals.
- Run the same creative for more than 4 weeks without refresh.
- Target competitors' brand keywords in search ads (company policy).
- Use humor that could be misinterpreted across cultures.

## Compliance Notes
- All ads must include: "NovaByte Technologies Pte. Ltd." in the advertiser field.
- Financial ROI claims must link to the case study source.
- GDPR consent must be collected before any EU retargeting.
- All landing pages must load within 3 seconds on mobile.

## Target Audiences
- Primary: VP/Director of Analytics, Head of Data, CTO at companies with 200-2000 employees.
- Secondary: Marketing Directors seeking attribution solutions.
- Tertiary: CFOs evaluating ROI on technology investments.
- Exclusions: Students, job seekers, companies under 50 employees.
