## [voice] Brand voice
Warm, calm and specific. Describe what a guest will see and do: the harbour view at dusk, the rooftop garden, the walk to the food street. Avoid hype words.

## [price-messaging] Price messaging in headlines
topic: price-claims
position: validity-period-omitted-in-headline
Lead headlines with the "from" nightly rate only. Keep dates and booking conditions off the headline and put them on the landing page, so the headline stays short.

## [imagery] Imagery
Use real photography of the property. Do not use stock photos of guests. Night shots must be clearly marked as such.

## [audiences] Audiences
Primary audiences are leisure travellers planning a city stay of two to four nights, and families visiting during school holidays.
